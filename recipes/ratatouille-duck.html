French Provençal Ratatouille with Herb-Seared Duck Breast

Crisp-skinned duck breast over slow-cooked Provençal ratatouille, finished with fresh basil.

Prep 15 min (est.)Cook 40 min (est.)Serves 48g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 2 large boneless duck breasts (about 12 oz each)
  • 1 medium eggplant
  • 2 zucchini
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 yellow onion
  • 3 Roma tomatoes
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • Fresh thyme
  • Fresh basil
  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Herbes de Provence
  • Sea salt
  • Cracked black pepper

Steps

  1. Render the Duck: Score duck skin in a crosshatch pattern without cutting into the meat. Place skin-side down in a cold skillet, turn heat to medium-low, and render fat for 15 minutes until skin is mahogany and crisp. Flip for 3 minutes for medium-rare; rest 10 minutes before slicing.
  2. Simmer Ratatouille: Dice eggplant, zucchini, bell pepper, onion, and tomatoes into uniform ½-inch cubes. Sauté onion and garlic in 1 tablespoon rendered duck fat, add remaining vegetables and 1 teaspoon herbes de Provence, and simmer covered for 20 minutes until tender. Finish with torn basil.
