Spanish Garlic Shrimp (Gambas al Ajillo) with Shaved Fennel & Asparagus

Sizzling garlic shrimp in smoked-paprika olive oil, with crisp asparagus and shaved fennel.

Prep 10 min (est.)Cook 10 min (est.)Serves 46g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs large wild shrimp (peeled and deveined, tails on)
  • 1 bunch thin asparagus (woody ends snapped)
  • 1 bulb fresh fennel (cored and shaved paper-thin)
  • 6 cloves garlic (thinly sliced)
  • 1 lemon
  • Flat-leaf parsley
  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Dry sherry (optional, 2 tbsp)
  • Smoked Spanish paprika (pimentón)
  • Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. Toss the Salad: Toss shaved fennel with lemon juice, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and sea salt so it softens slightly while cooking the shrimp.
  2. Blister Asparagus: Sear asparagus in a hot skillet with 1 teaspoon olive oil for 3–4 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender; plate alongside fennel.
  3. Sizzle the Shrimp: In the same skillet, warm ¼ cup olive oil over medium heat. Add sliced garlic and red pepper flakes; cook 60 seconds until garlic is fragrant (not brown). Add shrimp and 1 tsp smoked paprika, cooking 2 minutes per side until opaque. Deglaze with sherry, toss with parsley, and spoon garlic oil over the shrimp and asparagus.
