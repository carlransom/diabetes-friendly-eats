Japanese Tamagoyaki (Rolled Dashi Omelet) with Miso-Glazed Salmon

Delicate rolled dashi omelet with miso-glazed salmon and grated daikon.

Prep 10 min (est.)Cook 15 min (est.)Serves 43g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 4 wild salmon fillets (4 oz each, skin-on)
  • 3 scallions (thinly sliced)
  • 2-inch piece daikon radish (grated)
  • 1-inch fresh ginger (grated)
  • 1 cup dashi stock (or water + ½ tsp hondashi granules)
  • White miso paste
  • Tamari
  • Toasted sesame oil
  • Liquid monk fruit extract (zero-carb)
  • Avocado oil

Steps

  1. Bake the Salmon: Whisk 1 tablespoon white miso, 1 teaspoon tamari, 1 teaspoon sesame oil, grated ginger, and 3 drops liquid monk fruit. Brush over salmon fillets and broil on high for 6–7 minutes until caramelized.
  2. Whisk the Tamagoyaki: Gently beat eggs with ½ cup dashi stock, 1 teaspoon tamari, and 4 drops liquid monk fruit (avoid over-whisking to prevent air bubbles).
  3. Roll the Omelet: Lightly oil a rectangular tamagoyaki pan or 8-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat. Pour in a thin layer of egg; as it sets, roll it to one side of the pan. Oil the empty space, pour in more egg (lifting the roll so raw egg flows underneath), and roll again. Repeat 3–4 times, slice into medallions, and serve alongside salmon and grated daikon.
