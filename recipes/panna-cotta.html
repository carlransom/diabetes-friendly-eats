Italian Espresso & Mascarpone Panna Cotta

Silky espresso-mascarpone custard, set with gelatin and dusted with cocoa.

Prep 15 min (est.)Cook 10 min (est.) · chill 3 hrsServes 43g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 8 oz full-fat mascarpone cheese (room temperature)
  • ½ cup freshly brewed espresso (cooled slightly)
  • 1 packet (2 ¼ tsp) unflavored grass-fed gelatin powder
  • 3 tablespoons powdered allulose or erythritol-monk fruit blend
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon unsweetened Dutch-process cocoa powder
  • Pinch of sea salt

Steps

  1. Bloom the Gelatin: Pour warm espresso into a small bowl, sprinkle gelatin evenly over the surface, and let stand for 5 minutes until spongy.
  2. Infuse the Cream: In a saucepan over low heat, warm heavy cream, powdered sweetener, vanilla, and sea salt until steam rises (do not boil). Remove from heat and whisk in the bloomed gelatin until completely dissolved.
  3. Emulsify & Chill: Whisk the warm cream mixture gradually into the room-temperature mascarpone until velvety smooth. Divide into 4 ramekins, chill for at least 3 hours until set, and dust lightly with unsweetened cocoa powder before serving.
