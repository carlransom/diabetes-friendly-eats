Mediterranean Cauliflower Rice Bowls

Lemon chicken over cauliflower rice with cucumber, tomato, olives and feta. Bright, fresh, and filling.

Prep 10 minCook 12 minServes 410g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, cubed
  • 4 cups cauliflower rice
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup kalamata olives
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Season chicken with oregano, salt and pepper; cook in 1 tbsp oil until browned.
  2. Sauté cauliflower rice in remaining oil 4–5 minutes; season with half the lemon juice.
  3. Whisk tahini with remaining lemon juice and 2 tbsp water.
  4. Build bowls: cauliflower rice, chicken, cucumber, tomatoes, olives, feta.
  5. Drizzle with tahini-lemon sauce.
