Air Fryer Chicken Fajita Bowls

Charred peppers and onions, juicy chicken, cauliflower rice underneath. The air fryer does the work in 12 minutes.

Prep 10 minCook 12 minServes 410g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken breast, sliced into strips
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 4 cups cauliflower rice
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Steps

  1. Heat air fryer to 400°F.
  2. Toss chicken, peppers and onion with oil and fajita seasoning.
  3. Cook in a single layer 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until chicken is cooked through.
  4. While it cooks, sauté cauliflower rice 4 minutes with salt.
  5. Build bowls and top with avocado and lime.
