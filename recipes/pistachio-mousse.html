Middle Eastern Cardamom & Rosewater Pistachio Cheesecake Mousse

A no-bake whipped cream cheese and labneh mousse scented with cardamom and rosewater, topped with toasted pistachios.

Prep 15 min (est.)Cook 5 min (est.) · chill 30 minServes 45g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 8 oz full-fat cream cheese (softened)
  • ½ cup heavy whipping cream
  • ¼ cup full-fat Greek yogurt or labneh
  • ⅓ cup shelled raw pistachios (finely chopped)
  • 3 tablespoons powdered monk fruit/allulose sweetener
  • ½ teaspoon freshly ground green cardamom
  • ½ teaspoon culinary rosewater (or orange blossom water)
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Whip the Base: Beat softened cream cheese, Greek yogurt, powdered sweetener, ground cardamom, rosewater, and vanilla with a hand mixer for 2 minutes until completely smooth and aerated.
  2. Fold in Cream: In a separate chilled bowl, whip heavy cream to stiff peaks. Gently fold the whipped cream into the spiced cream cheese mixture in two additions to preserve volume.
  3. Toast & Serve: Toast chopped pistachios in a dry skillet over medium heat for 2 minutes until fragrant. Spoon mousse into 4 coupe glasses, chill 30 minutes, and top with warm toasted pistachios.
