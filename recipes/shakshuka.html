Levantine Shakshuka with Whipped Labneh & Za'atar

Eggs poached in a spiced, slow-simmered tomato and red pepper sauce, finished with whipped labneh and za'atar.

Prep 10 min (est.)Cook 20 min (est.)Serves 47g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 8 large pasture-raised eggs
  • ½ cup full-fat labneh (or strained Greek yogurt)
  • 1 red bell pepper (diced)
  • 1 medium yellow onion (diced)
  • 4 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 jalapeño (seeded and minced)
  • 1 bunch fresh cilantro
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed San Marzano tomatoes (no sugar added)
  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Ground cumin
  • Smoked paprika
  • Cayenne pepper
  • Za'atar spice blend
  • Sea salt

Steps

  1. Build the Base: Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a wide, deep skillet over medium heat. Sauté onion and bell pepper for 6 minutes until softened. Add garlic, jalapeño, 1 ½ teaspoons cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, and a pinch of cayenne; toast for 60 seconds.
  2. Simmer & Poach: Pour in crushed tomatoes and salt; simmer for 10 minutes until thickened. Make 8 shallow wells with the back of a spoon, crack one egg into each well, cover, and cook 5–7 minutes until whites are set and yolks remain runny.
  3. Garnish: Dollop cold labneh over the warm sauce, scatter with za'atar, cilantro, and parsley, and serve immediately.
