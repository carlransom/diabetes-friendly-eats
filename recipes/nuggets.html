Crispy Air Fryer Chicken Nuggets

Almond-flour crust, real chicken, zero drive-through. Kids eat them too — that's the point.

Prep 10 minCook 12 minServes 44g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • Salt and pepper
  • Olive oil spray

Steps

  1. Heat air fryer to 400°F.
  2. Mix almond flour, parmesan, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Dip chicken in egg, then coat in the almond mixture.
  4. Spray the basket, arrange nuggets in one layer, spray tops lightly.
  5. Cook 10–12 minutes, flipping once, until golden and cooked through.
