Everything Bagel Deviled Eggs

The classic, with a yogurt swap and the seasoning everyone steals from the bagel shop.

Prep 10 minCook 12 minServes 6 (2 halves each)1g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 3 tbsp plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp everything bagel seasoning, plus more to top
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Cover eggs with cold water; bring to a boil, then cover and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Cool in ice water and peel.
  3. Halve, scoop yolks into a bowl.
  4. Mash yolks with yogurt, mustard, seasoning, salt and pepper.
  5. Spoon back into whites and dust with more seasoning.
