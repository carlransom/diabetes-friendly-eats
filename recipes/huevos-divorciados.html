Mexican Huevos Divorciados over Crispy Chicharrón & Avocado

Crispy pork rinds topped with fried eggs, red and green salsas, avocado and queso fresco.

Prep 15 min (est.)Cook 15 min (est.)Serves 46g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 3 oz plain pork rinds (chicharrónes)
  • ½ cup crumbled queso fresco
  • 6 tomatillos (husked and rinsed)
  • 3 Roma tomatoes
  • 2 ripe avocados (sliced)
  • 1 white onion
  • 2 jalapeños
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 2 limes
  • Avocado oil
  • Dried ancho chili (or 1 tsp ancho chili powder)
  • Sea salt

Steps

  1. Roast the Dual Salsas: Broil tomatillos, Roma tomatoes, jalapeños, half the onion, and unpeeled garlic on a sheet pan for 8 minutes until blistered. Blend tomatillos with 1 jalapeño, half the cilantro, lime juice, and salt for salsa verde. Blend tomatoes with the remaining jalapeño, ancho chili, garlic, and salt for salsa roja.
  2. Fry the Eggs: Fry 8 eggs sunny-side up in avocado oil until edges are lacy and crisp.
  3. Plate: Divide pork rinds across 4 plates, top each with two eggs, ladle salsa verde over one egg and salsa roja over the other, and garnish with sliced avocado, queso fresco, and minced onion.
