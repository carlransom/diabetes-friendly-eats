No-Bake Lemon Cheesecake Bites

Bright, creamy little squares with an almond crust. No oven, no sugar, all dessert.

Prep 15 minCook Chill 2 hrsMakes 12 bites4g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 3 tbsp butter, melted
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup monk fruit sweetener
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Mix almond flour and melted butter; press into a lined 8-inch square pan.
  2. Beat cream cheese, yogurt, sweetener, lemon zest and juice, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Spread over the crust.
  4. Chill at least 2 hours.
  5. Cut into 12 squares and serve cold.
