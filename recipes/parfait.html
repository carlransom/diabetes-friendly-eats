Greek Yogurt Berry Parfait

Five minutes, one glass, no cooking. Thick yogurt, berries, chia and walnuts — sweet enough to feel like a treat.

Prep 5 minCook 0 minServes 118g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup mixed berries
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 2 tbsp chopped walnuts
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • A few drops of monk fruit sweetener (optional)

Steps

  1. Stir vanilla and sweetener into the yogurt.
  2. Layer half the yogurt in a glass, add half the berries and chia.
  3. Repeat the layers.
  4. Top with walnuts and serve.
