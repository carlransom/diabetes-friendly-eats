Turkish Grilled Chicken Adana Kebabs with Sumac Onion Salad

Char-grilled spiced chicken kebabs with tangy sumac-rubbed red onions.

Prep 20 min (est.)Cook 10 min (est.)Serves 45g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground chicken (preferably dark meat or a mix of breast and thigh for moisture)
  • 1 red bell pepper (finely minced and squeezed dry)
  • 1 large red onion (halved and thinly sliced)
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 lemon
  • Ground sumac
  • Aleppo pepper (or mild chili flakes)
  • Ground cumin
  • Olive oil
  • Wooden or metal skewers

Steps

  1. Season & Skewer: Combine ground chicken, squeezed red bell pepper, minced garlic, finely chopped parsley, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp Aleppo pepper, and salt. Knead for 2 minutes until tacky, then mold onto flat skewers in elongated ribbons.
  2. Make Sumac Onions: Rub sliced red onions with 1 tablespoon ground sumac, a pinch of salt, lemon juice, and remaining parsley leaves until softened and vibrant magenta.
  3. Grill: Grill or broil skewers over high heat for 4–5 minutes per side until charred outside and cooked through (165°F internal temperature). Serve immediately over the sumac onion salad.
