All-American Jalapeño Popper Bacon Cheeseburger Skillet

A creamy one-skillet cheeseburger with crispy bacon, jalapeño heat, melted sharp cheddar and tangy pickles, served over crisp lettuce.

Prep 10 min (est.)Cook 20 min (est.)Serves 44g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs Laura's Lean 92% Lean Ground Beef (or Certified Angus Beef ground chuck)
  • 6 slices Oscar Mayer Center Cut Bacon (chopped)
  • 4 oz Philadelphia Original Cream Cheese (cubed, room temperature)
  • 1 cup Tillamook Sharp Cheddar Cheese (shredded)
  • 2 tbsp Land O'Lakes Unsalted Butter
  • 2 fresh jalapeño peppers (seeded, finely diced)
  • 1 head Dole Iceberg or Romaine Lettuce
  • 4 Mount Olive Kosher Dill Pickle Spears (diced)
  • 3 tbsp Heinz Tomato Ketchup with No Sugar Added
  • 1 tbsp French's Classic Yellow Mustard
  • 1 tbsp Hellmann's Real Mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp McCormick Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp McCormick Onion Powder
  • Morton Kosher Salt & McCormick Coarse Ground Black Pepper

Steps

  1. Crisp the Bacon: Cook chopped Oscar Mayer bacon in a large cast-iron skillet over medium heat for 6–7 minutes until crisp. Transfer bacon to a paper towel-lined plate, leaving 1 tablespoon of drippings in the pan.
  2. Brown the Beef & Peppers: Increase heat to medium-high. Add ground beef, diced jalapeños, McCormick garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper. Sear undisturbed for 3 minutes to develop a deep crust, then break apart and cook until browned; drain excess fat.
  3. Build the Burger Sauce & Melt: Reduce heat to low. Stir in Land O'Lakes butter, cubed Philadelphia cream cheese, Heinz No Sugar Added Ketchup, French's yellow mustard, and Hellmann's mayonnaise until smooth and creamy. Fold in half the cooked bacon and top with Tillamook cheddar; cover for 2 minutes until melted.
  4. Serve: Garnish with the remaining crispy bacon and diced Mount Olive dill pickles, serving over shredded Dole lettuce or scooped into crisp lettuce cups.
