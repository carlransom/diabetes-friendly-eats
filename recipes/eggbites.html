Baked Spinach & Feta Egg Bites

Grab-and-go egg muffins that keep all week. Spinach, feta, and enough protein to hold you till lunch.

Prep 10 minCook 18 minServes 6 (2 bites each)3g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup cottage cheese
  • 1 cup fresh spinach, chopped
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • Nonstick spray

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 350°F; grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
  2. Whisk eggs, cottage cheese, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Stir in spinach, feta and tomatoes.
  4. Divide among cups.
  5. Bake 16–18 minutes until set and lightly golden.
  6. Cool 5 minutes, then lift out. Keeps in the fridge up to 5 days.
