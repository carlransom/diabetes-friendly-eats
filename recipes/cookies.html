Almond Flour Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft in the middle, crisp at the edges, sweetened without sugar. The cookie jar is back.

Prep 10 minCook 12 minMakes 12 cookies5g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 2 cups almond flour
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup monk fruit sweetener
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup sugar-free chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 350°F; line a baking sheet.
  2. Beat butter and sweetener until fluffy.
  3. Beat in egg and vanilla.
  4. Stir in almond flour, baking soda and salt, then fold in chips.
  5. Scoop 12 balls, flatten slightly.
  6. Bake 11–12 minutes until edges are golden; cool on the pan.
