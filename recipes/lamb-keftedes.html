Greek Lamb Keftedes with Tzatziki & Marinated Artichoke Salad

Herb-flecked lamb meatballs with cool tzatziki and a bright marinated artichoke salad.

Prep 20 min (est.)Cook 20 min (est.)Serves 47g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground lamb
  • 1 cup full-fat strained Greek yogurt (2% or 5%)
  • 1 English cucumber
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bunch fresh mint
  • 1 bunch fresh dill
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 jar marinated artichoke hearts (in olive oil/water, no sugar)
  • Kalamata olives
  • Dried oregano
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Form & Bake Keftedes: Grate half the red onion and squeeze out excess liquid. Mix with ground lamb, 2 minced garlic cloves, chopped mint, 1 tsp dried oregano, ¼ tsp cinnamon, salt, and pepper. Roll into 16 meatballs and roast at 400°F (200°C) for 16–18 minutes.
  2. Prepare Tzatziki: Grate half the cucumber, salt it, and squeeze dry in a clean towel. Mix with Greek yogurt, 1 grated garlic clove, chopped dill, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and juice of 1 lemon.
  3. Assemble: Toss drained artichoke hearts, halved Kalamata olives, diced remaining cucumber, and lemon juice as a bright side salad alongside the meatballs and tzatziki.
