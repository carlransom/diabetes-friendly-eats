Brazilian Moqueca Baiana (Coconut-Lime Snapper Stew)

Bahian coconut-lime stew with tender snapper, sweet peppers and cilantro.

Prep 15 min (est., plus 15 min marinating)Cook 15 min (est.)Serves 47g carbs / serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs firm white fish (red snapper, halibut, or cod), cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 yellow bell pepper
  • 1 medium yellow onion
  • 2 ripe tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 2 limes
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) unsweetened coconut milk
  • 1 tablespoon red palm oil (or olive oil + ½ tsp annatto/paprika)
  • Sea salt

Steps

  1. Marinate Fish: Toss fish chunks with lime juice, minced garlic, and sea salt; let sit for 15 minutes while prepping vegetables.
  2. Layer the Stew: Thinly slice onion, bell peppers, and tomatoes into rings. Heat red palm oil in a heavy Dutch oven, layer half the vegetables, top with fish, and cover with remaining vegetables.
  3. Simmer: Pour coconut milk over the top, cover tightly, and simmer gently over medium-low heat for 12–15 minutes until fish flakes easily. Scatter generously with chopped cilantro before serving.
